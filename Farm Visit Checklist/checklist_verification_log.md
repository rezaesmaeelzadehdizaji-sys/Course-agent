## Corrections applied this pass (full checklist only)

1. **Litter score 1-4 → 0-4.** No standard system uses 1-4. The Welfare Quality
   litter scale runs 0 (dry, flaky) to 4 (caked). Range and descriptor aligned.
2. **Light "first 7 days ≥20 lux" → "≥20 lux, NFACC first 3 days."** NFACC's
   regulatory minimum is 20 lux for the first 3 days, then adequate light
   (about 5-10 lux). The 7-day figure was Aviagen best-practice, not the
   Canadian standard the form references.
3. **Dark period "≥6 h" → "≥4 continuous hours per 24 h (NFACC min; many give 6+)."**
   NFACC requires at least 4 straight hours of darkness from about day 5. 6 h is
   a common best-practice target but not the regulatory minimum.

## Note on lighting figures (Aviagen vs NFACC)

Aviagen best-practice recommends keeping ~20 lux through roughly the first week
and giving 6+ hours of darkness. NFACC sets the regulatory floor (20 lux first 3
days; 4 h darkness). The checklist references the NFACC Code of Practice, so the
NFACC minimums are used. If the clinic prefers to present the Aviagen
best-practice targets instead, both are legitimate — say which and the forms can
be adjusted.

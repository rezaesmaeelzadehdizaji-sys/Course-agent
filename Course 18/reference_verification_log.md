## Terminology note (emerging vs re-emerging)

Section 3 covers both. DMV/1639 is presented as the re-emerging case, matching the
definition given in 3.1: an old foe returning in a changed form that the usual vaccines do
not fully cover. IBH is presented as emerging, matching its cited source [37] ("IBH and HHS
are currently considered emerging poultry diseases") and the Canadian literature (Gomis et
al. 2006, Avian Dis 50(4):550-5, PMID 17274293: "IBH has emerged as an economically
important disease in Western Canada"). Reference 38 uses neither term. "Reemerging" does
appear for IBH in international literature, but no Canadian source applies it, so the course
follows its sources.

## Terminology note (emerging vs re-emerging)

Section 3 covers both. The definition in 3.1 now rests on the WOAH glossary [19], whose
emerging-disease definition includes "a change of a known pathogenic agent"; the course glosses
that case as re-emerging. DMV/1639 is presented as the re-emerging case: an old foe returning
in a changed form that the usual vaccines do not fully cover. IBH is presented as emerging,
matching its cited source [22] ("IBH and HHS are currently considered emerging poultry
diseases") and the Canadian literature (Gomis et al. 2006, Avian Dis 50(4):550-5, PMID
17274293: "IBH has emerged as an economically important disease in Western Canada").
Reference 23 uses neither term. "Reemerging" does appear for IBH in international literature,
but no Canadian source applies it, so the course follows its sources.
